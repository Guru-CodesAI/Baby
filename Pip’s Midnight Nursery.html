    How the dreams began.

    The first spark

    One sleepy evening, Pip found a lonely little star hiding beneath a nursery window. It was too shy to shine, so Pip gathered a soft cloud and built it a tiny lantern.

    ♪♫♪

    The first melody

    That evening, the little star began to hum. Pip listened carefully, collected the notes, and tucked them inside a music box. By midnight, the whole neighborhood had something gentle to dream about.

    The first delivery

    With a lantern in one hand and a lullaby in the other, Pip set off across the rooftops. By sunrise, every tiny window glowed softly. The Midnight Nursery had found its smallest, sleepiest dream engineer.
